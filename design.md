# Kartavia Design System

## Concept
Kartavia is a premium tourism platform for the Jogja region, heavily inspired by Klook and Atourin. The design prioritizes a clean, spacious, and modern aesthetic to let the vibrant photos of Jogja's destinations stand out.

## Color Palette
- **Base (Background):** White (`#FFFFFF`) - used extensively to create a clean, minimalist feel.
- **Surface:** Off-white (`#F8FAFC`) - used for subtle cards and backgrounds.
- **Primary:** Klook-inspired Orange (`#FF5A5F`) or deep Blue (`#0066CC`) - used for primary calls-to-action (CTAs). For Kartavia, we will use a vibrant coral/orange as the primary accent to evoke energy and excitement.
- **Text (Primary):** Dark Gray (`#1E293B`) - for high readability.
- **Text (Secondary):** Slate Gray (`#64748B`) - for descriptions and less important text.

## Typography
- **Font Family:** Inter or Roboto (via Google Fonts).
- **Headings:** Bold, clean, using high contrast against the white background.
- **Body:** Regular weight, ensuring high readability.

## UI Components
### 1. Navigation Bar
- Transparent background at the top (hero section), transitioning to a solid white background with a subtle shadow on scroll.
- Minimalist links.
- Prominent "Sign In" or "Book Now" buttons.

### 2. Hero Section
- Large, high-quality background image or a clean white section with a prominent search bar.
- The search bar will have inputs for "Destination", "Dates", and "Guests".

### 3. Cards (Destinations, Tours, Rentals)
- White background with subtle shadows (`shadow-sm`, hover `shadow-md`).
- Rounded corners (`rounded-xl` or `rounded-2xl`).
- Image at the top, content at the bottom.
- Hover effects: subtle upward translation (`-translate-y-1`) and image scale (`scale-105`).

### 4. Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid color (Primary accent), rounded corners, white text.
- **Secondary:** Outline or light gray background with dark text.

## Micro-animations
- Smooth transitions on all interactive elements (`transition-all duration-300`).
- Hover effects on cards and buttons.
- Fade-in animations for page load.
